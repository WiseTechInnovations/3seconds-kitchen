Our story

Fiapre's flavour stop.

Welcome to 3 Seconds Kitchen, a food spot at Fiapre – UENR Roundabout, bringing together local favourites and much-loved quick bites.

Our menu offers something for every mood — from jollof, fried rice, banku and fufu to pizza, yam chips, spaghetti and more. Whether you're eating alone or treating your people, there's something to look forward to.

Come by, take a look at the menu, or reach out to arrange a meal for delivery or pickup.
